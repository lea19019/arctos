## Most important (20)

The papers to know before the rest: the phenomenon (massive activations, sinks), the multilingual side, and the translation side. Newest first.

Peer reviewed:

- [Subramanian-2026-Super-Weights-Failure-of-Selective-Training](../papers/Subramanian-2026-Super-Weights-Failure-of-Selective-Training.pdf) — COLM 2026 — Direct follow-up: training super weights in isolation fails at chance; Table 8 reports **no effect** for top-magnitude coordinates in four models — the negative this project's joint-set finding explains.
- [An-2025-Systematic-Outliers-in-LLMs](../papers/An-2025-Systematic-Outliers-in-LLMs.pdf) — ICLR 2025 — Unifies weight / activation / attention outliers into one causal chain; argues they are implicit **context-aware scaling factors** arising from softmax's inability to express a zero update.
- [Gu-2025-When-Attention-Sink-Emerges](../papers/Gu-2025-When-Attention-Sink-Emerges.pdf) — ICLR 2025 — The pretraining-knob study: LR, weight decay, data volume, packing, loss, positional encoding, and the sigmoid-without-normalisation result. Defines the `Sink^ε_k` metric everyone else reuses.
- [Barbero-2025-Why-LLMs-Attend-First-Token](../papers/Barbero-2025-Why-LLMs-Attend-First-Token.pdf) — COLM 2025 — Sinks stop attention from over-mixing; context length controls them; removing BOS from Gemma-7B collapses benchmarks.
- [QueipoDeLlano-2025-Sinks-Compression-Valleys-Same-Coin](../papers/QueipoDeLlano-2025-Sinks-Compression-Valleys-Same-Coin.pdf) — ICLR 2026 — The opposing causal claim: ablating the layer-0 MLP contribution to BOS in a *pretrained* model kills sinks and the compression valley together (Thm 1, Fig. 4).
- [Achilles-2025-Altering-Neurons-Cripples-Language](../papers/Achilles-2025-Altering-Neurons-Cripples-Language.pdf) — ICLR 2026 — 3–45 neurons whose joint masking collapses any of 21 models; any subset is harmless, the full set is fatal; same neurons across five input languages.
- [Bafna-2025-Translation-Barrier-Hypothesis](../papers/Bafna-2025-Translation-Barrier-Hypothesis.pdf) — IJCNLP-AACL 2025 — On Aya-8B and Llama-3.1-8B, >90% of correct word translations are already correct in some language mid-model; English is only 30–48% of them; the final translate step fails most for low-resource targets.
- [Sun-2024-COLM-Massive-Activations-in-LLMs](../papers/Sun-2024-COLM-Massive-Activations-in-LLMs.pdf) — COLM 2024 — Defines massive activations; the zero-vs-mean intervention (Table 3) that makes the "fixed bias" claim; shows explicit learnable KV biases remove the need for them.
- [Stolfo-2024-Confidence-Regulation-Neurons](../papers/Stolfo-2024-Confidence-Regulation-Neurons.pdf) — NeurIPS 2024 — Entropy neurons (via the final norm) and token-frequency neurons (unigram direction) regulate confidence; the frozen-norm ablation tool.
- [Wendler-2024-Do-Llamas-Work-in-English](../papers/Wendler-2024-Do-Llamas-Work-in-English.pdf) — ACL 2024 — Llama-2's middle layers lean toward the English token before switching to the target language late; interpreted as a concept space biased toward English.
- [Tang-2024-Language-Specific-Neurons-LAPE](../papers/Tang-2024-Language-Specific-Neurons-LAPE.pdf) — ACL 2024 — LAPE: language-specific neurons by activation entropy; U-shape over layers; English has the fewest; forcing them steers output language.
- [Cancedda-2024-Spectral-Filters-Dark-Signals-Attention-Sinks](../papers/Cancedda-2024-Spectral-Filters-Dark-Signals-Attention-Sinks.pdf) — ACL 2024 (long — An early MLP writes a 'dark' vector into the BOS stream that implements sinking; filtering it yields 'the, the, the…' output, the failure this track studies.
- [Koishekenov-2023-NLLB200-Language-Specific-Expert-Pruning](../papers/Koishekenov-2023-NLLB200-Language-Specific-Expert-Pruning.pdf) — ACL 2023 — NLLB-200 decoder experts are language-specific, encoder experts are not; 80% pruned at almost no cost; pruning increases repetition.
- [Guerreiro-2023-Hallucinations-Large-Multilingual-Translation](../papers/Guerreiro-2023-Hallucinations-Large-Multilingual-Translation.pdf) — TACL 2023 — Hallucination rates across 100+ directions in M2M-100/SMaLL100/NLLB: far higher in low-resource pairs and out of English; NLLB family untested for causes.
- [Xiao-2023-Efficient-Streaming-LMs-Attention-Sinks](../papers/Xiao-2023-Efficient-Streaming-LMs-Attention-Sinks.pdf) — ICLR 2024 — Names attention sinks; keeping the first tokens' KV lets windowed attention stream indefinitely; one learnable sink token suffices.
- [Dettmers-2022-LLM-int8](../papers/Dettmers-2022-LLM-int8.pdf) — NeurIPS 2022 — Origin of "outlier feature"; §4.1's criterion (magnitude ≥ 6.0, ≥25% of layers, ≥6% of tokens) is the field's most-copied threshold — and was reverse-engineered to yield one detection in a 125M model.

Preprints:

- [Owen-2025-Refined-Analysis-Massive-Activations](../papers/Owen-2025-Refined-Analysis-Massive-Activations.pdf) — preprint — **Read this before writing any ablation number.** 17 models with BOS controlled: set-to-mean is harmless in all 16, set-to-zero is catastrophic in only 9, OLMo-2-7B has no massive activations at all, and DyT removes them while attention concentration persists.
- [Interpreting-2025-Repeated-Token-Phenomenon](../papers/Interpreting-2025-Repeated-Token-Phenomenon.pdf) — preprint — Yona et al.: one or two early 'sink neurons' build the sink (Llama-2-7B layer 1 neuron 7890, this track's TowerBase super weight); repeated tokens fool the first-token detector and generation diverges.
- [Tracing-Multilingual-Representations-CrossLayer-Transcoders-2025](../papers/Tracing-Multilingual-Representations-CrossLayer-Transcoders-2025.pdf) — preprint — Trains 68M–177M models with 90%→0% English: language-specific features early and late, shared in the middle, even with no English; a few always-on features set the output language.
- [Marchisio-2024-Quantization-Multilingual-LLMs](../papers/Marchisio-2024-Quantization-Multilingual-LLMs.pdf) — Findings of EMNLP 2024 — Quantization hurts non-Latin scripts and low-data languages more; humans see damage automatic metrics miss.

## Why quantization hurts low-resource languages (25)

Papers behind `docs/literature/notes_lrl_quantization_why.md`: per-language compression effects, the long-tail line, quantization vs confidence, data size vs PTQ robustness, and tokenizer fragmentation. Newest first.
