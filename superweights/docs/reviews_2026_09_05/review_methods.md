# Methods review — super-weight collapse project (proposal draft 2, 2026-09-05)

## (a) Verdict: SURVIVES WITH CHANGES

The lesion is real and cheap (5 catastrophic coordinates, ×16–×3667 on wikitext-2, one replicated against Yu and one against Subramanian), the tooling is half-built, and two experiments (neuron-granularity ablation against a magnitude-matched null; frozen-final-norm + KL-to-unigram) are clean and decisive. But the plan as written is ~200 h of work in a 125–150 h budget, its central control ("set to mean" for a *weight*) is undefined as stated, its "criterion with a false-positive rate" is calibrated on either a trivial null (step 0) or the wrong statistic (|W|), and RQ2(b)'s headline correlation is confounded by generic fragility in a way the proposal's own control cannot remove. Nothing here has n>1 in the repo's sense (seeds); every model-level claim is a case study and must be written as one.

## (b) Objections

**1. "Set to mean" is undefined for a scalar weight; the control triple conflates two interventions.** Owen 2025 Table 1 and Sun 2024 Table 3 set the *activation* to its mean over positions. A weight is already a constant; its "mean over a calibration corpus" is itself. The parked `ablate_sw.py` has zero, random-top-100 weight, and SA-removal — no mean arm exists. *Fix (cheap, and it is the mediation test you want):* replace the scalar's *contribution* `W[j,k]·x_k` at `down_proj` output with its calibration-corpus mean (a learned bias), via a forward hook. If that is harmless while zeroing is catastrophic, the weight is a bias generator and H-prior/H-sink become questions about the bias, not the weight.

**2. RQ3's null is either trivial or answers the wrong question.** Step-0 checkpoints have no persistent residual spikes, so the persistence detector returns nothing — FPR ≈ 0 by construction. Within-matrix permutation of |W| calibrates magnitude outlierness, and the repo has already shown 16/21 rank-1–6 coordinates are individually inert: a well-calibrated |W| null flags them anyway. Planting a weight tests recall for a weight shaped like the detector's own assumption. *Fix:* define the criterion on the causal quantity — ablation ratio vs. the max-statistic over N≥50 magnitude-matched random weights from the same matrix (parked `--null-n` design, ~25 GPU-min/model). State honestly that Llama-2 ×1.55 and TowerBase ×1.58 sit at the null ceiling (parked probe uses <×1.5) and are undecidable, not "inert".

**3. RQ2(b)'s covariate test is confounded by fragility.** Low-resource languages degrade more under *any* perturbation — the repo's own PTQ result is that fact. A ×1.0 random-weight control has no profile to compare against, so "SW damage correlates with PTQ damage" is near-guaranteed and uninformative. With n=20 languages (non-independent: scripts, families), the 95% CI on ρ is ≈±0.4; partialling three collinear covariates leaves ~16 df; the repo's PTQ covariate covers 6 languages, not 20. *Fix:* a damage-matched control (Gaussian noise on the same `down_proj`, scaled to the same aggregate COMET/PPL drop; or the 4-bit quantized model already in-repo) and report the profile *residual*. Call the correlation exploratory; the defensible deliverable is the profile with CIs, not the predictor.

**4. Full ablation floors every graded metric, and forced-decode KL does not rescue the *profile*.** At ×1430 the output is "without without. . ."; chrF++ and COMET floor for every language. KL(intact‖ablated) under teacher forcing, if the collapsed distribution is input-agnostic (the premise), reduces to the intact model's per-language cross-entropy against a fixed distribution — i.e., fertility and per-language confidence again. *Fix:* the dose–response sweep is the graded measure: per language, the weight scale α at which chrF++ falls to 50% of intact. Defined at all damage levels, tokenizer-free. Cost: 6 doses × n=200 sentences.

**5. RQ2(a) as written measures a point mass.** Greedy output after ablation is one repeated token; rank-correlating a point mass against a unigram table is undefined. "Forcing a target language" by prompt on a ×1000-perplexity model does nothing (input-agnostic collapse is the hypothesis). *Fix:* teacher-force language-L references and average the model's full next-token distribution; correlate *that* against the per-tokenizer unigram estimate; run it at partial doses where context still matters. Note "We" is not English's unigram argmax — H-prior is already strained on OLMo-1B.

**6. Two RQ1 instruments are mis-specified.** (i) Δlogit regression on {all-ones, log-freq}: the all-ones direction is annihilated by softmax and does not represent final-norm rescaling, which is *multiplicative* (Δlogit ∝ intact logits). Regress on {intact centred logits, centred log-frequency}; they are collinear (models predict frequent tokens), so report partial R², and estimate log-frequency per tokenizer on one shared corpus with a second-corpus sensitivity check. (ii) "Destroy the sink by removing BOS" does not destroy the sink — Sun 2024: it relocates to whatever is first; Owen: Gemma has MAs only *with* BOS. Use an attention mask on position 0 for all queries instead. Frozen-RMSNorm is implementable cleanly (freeze the per-position rms to the intact value under paired forced decoding) and carries a sign prediction (removing norm should *sharpen*; Yu observed flattening) — keep it.

**7. Budget does not close.** Seven RQ1 instruments × 5 models (≈45 h to build and debug), RQ3 granularity sweep (≈20 h), RQ2(a) + LAPE contrast arm (≈20 h; LAPE needs a per-language activation-statistics pipeline), RQ2(b) (≈25 h human, GPU-days), NLLB adapter verification (≈15 h; 455 unverified lines), RQ4 gate (≈8 h), writing (≈30 h) ≈ 165–200 h. Cut RQ4, the LAPE arm, ALTI+/TNG, and RQ1's inter-position-similarity/induction items first.

**8. NLLB premise error and prior.** In HF NLLB `decoder_start_token_id = </s>`; the language tag is position **1**. This is a free positional-vs-identity dissociation, not a bug, but "position 0 by construction" is wrong. NLLB is non-GLU with LayerNorm; Owen found non-GLU models (GPT-2, OPT, Phi-2) unaffected by MA zeroing. Expect a bounded negative; budget it as a gate, not an arm.

## (c) Minimum viable version (ordered, ~130 h)

1. Harness: bias-replacement arm (obj. 1), magnitude-matched null with max-statistic (obj. 2), rep-n, bootstrap CIs, provenance, config-load test. ~20 h.
2. RQ3 core: scalar vs. `down_proj`-column vs. gate/up-row ablation × {zero, bias-mean, null} on the 12 cached models, wikitext-2. Report: does neuron-level ablation collapse every model? Coverage stated; no predictor test. ~20 h.
3. RQ1 on 5 models, four instruments: frozen-RMSNorm; KL-to-unigram vs. norm-matched random-residual null; corrected Δlogit regression; position-0 attention mask vs. MA patch-in (full column contribution), generation readout. Dose sweep as the ordering axis. ~35 h.
4. RQ2(a)/(b) merged on 2 models (TowerBase, EuroLLM): 20 FLORES languages × dose sweep, chrF++ collapse-dose per language, teacher-forced next-token distribution vs. per-tokenizer unigram, damage-matched noise control. Profile with CIs; covariates exploratory. ~25 h + GPU-days.
5. NLLB gate: detection per stack + per-position residual norms on 600M and 3.3B; ablate top candidate; `</s>` vs. tag position. ~10 h.
6. Writing. ~25 h.

## (d)

**Most likely uninterpretable:** RQ2(b) covariate correlation (fragility confound, n=20 collinear languages, floored metrics). **Most likely clean:** RQ3 neuron-granularity ablation against the magnitude-matched null — one forward-pass protocol, existing models, a yes/no per model, and it may dissolve question (2) outright.
