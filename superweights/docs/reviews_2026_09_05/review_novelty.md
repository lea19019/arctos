# Adversarial review — novelty and decidability (2026-09-05)

## (a) Verdict: SURVIVES WITH CHANGES

The phenomenon is settled and the "what does the constant do" question is more answered than the statement admits: Sun 2024 (Table 3) and Owen 2025 (Table 1, 16 models) show it is a bias whose *value* is irrelevant (set-to-mean harmless), Sun 2026 (arXiv:2603.05498) and Fesser 2026 (arXiv:2606.08105) give the functional account (near-constant implicit parameter; sinks as NOP/short-range gate), and Sun 2026 shows models trained without the spike lose nothing. What is genuinely open is narrower: (i) why the trained model loops on function words rather than emitting noise, (ii) the unit of criticality, and (iii) anything multilingual or encoder-decoder — where the search found nothing. Questions (i) and (iii) are real; question (2) as posed ("why some models survive") is not yet a question the project has data for. With the fixes below the project is publishable at workshop/short-paper scale; as written, several of its controls do not test what they claim to.

## (b) Strongest objections

**1. The lesion is a weight, but two of the three controls are activation-level, and one is incoherent.** Methods §4: "set to the mean over a calibration corpus." A weight has no corpus mean; the set-to-mean result that motivates the triple (Owen Table 1, Sun Table 3) is an *activation* intervention. Applied to a weight, "mean of the matrix" ≈ 0 — it is zeroing again. Fix: run the triple at the activation (zero / mean / random at the sink position) *and* the matched-random control at the weight; the 16 individually-inert rank-1–6 coordinates are already a natural weight-level control and should be named as such.

**2. The three accounts are not exclusive and the "one regression separates them" claim is false.** H-sink → over-copying is a context-dependent attention effect; it has no fixed direction in logit space, so a Δlogit regression onto {1, v_freq, residual} cannot detect it — it only splits H-scale from H-prior from "other." H-scale is already disfavoured on sign (Stolfo §3.3: removing norm *sharpens*; Yu reports a flat 9.0% argmax). Most likely outcome: H-prior and H-sink both partly true, H-scale small — which is what Cancedda 2024 §6.1 and Puccetti 2022 §4.1 jointly predict, and is then documentation, not discovery. Fix: pre-register the ordering claim (positional collapse precedes distributional collapse, or not) via the dose–response and patching arms, and state what result would count as "none of the three."

**3. The sink-destruction dissociation does not destroy the sink.** "Remove the BOS token" (Methods, RQ1) moves the massive activation and sink to whatever token is now first (Sun 2024 §2.2; Oh 2024 App. C: forms on the first token and delimiters). Only in Gemma does dropping BOS remove the activation (Owen §"BOS Token"), and Gemma is not in the model set. As designed the arm is a no-op in Llama/OLMo/Mistral. Fix: destroy the sink by zeroing the MLP's contribution to position 0 at the onset layer (Queipo-de-Llano 2025 §3.3 — sink rate 0.85→0.0, already validated), with generation as the readout.

**4. Question (2) conflates two things and the project has data for neither.** Owen's ~7/16 "unaffected" are *activation*-zeroing survivors; the project's "survivors" (Phi-3 ×1.0, Llama-2 ×1.55, Llama-3.1 ×1.02) are single-*scalar* survivors that Subramanian Table 8 (Phi-3 6 SWs jointly ×374) and the project's own column-clustering finding say are neuron-critical. So "why some survive" is, on present evidence, the unit question (RQ3) wearing a different hat. And once the unit is fixed, n≈12 models spanning ~6 families cannot separate any predictor (GLU, BOS convention, fan-out, profile shape) from family membership — every candidate is collinear with family. Fix: drop question (2) as a headline; report it as an exploratory table with family as a stated confound, after RQ3 decides the unit.

**5. B1 ("does the repeated token follow the target language") is diagnostic only for NLLB.** In Tower/EuroLLM/Aya the target language is set by prompt; a collapsed model that ignores context will emit the *model's* global prior ("the") in every condition. The proposal would read that as "pinned direction," but it is equally "fallback to the English-dominated corpus prior." The null must be the model's own unconditional unigram, not the target language's. NLLB is the one model where the target tag is structurally forced (decoder position 0), so B1 is well-posed there and nowhere else. Fix: run B1 on NLLB first; for decoder-only models add the model-global-unigram null or drop the arm.

**6. B2 (per-language damage) will saturate and then measure the baseline.** At ×100–×3000 perplexity every language collapses; chrF++ floors (the proposal concedes this) and forced-decode KL under collapse is dominated by the *intact* model's per-language entropy — i.e., it correlates with resource level by construction. Fix: measure per-language damage at sub-catastrophic dose (weight × 0.5, 0.25) where a profile can exist, and pre-register that the covariate test is on the dose–response slope, not on the collapsed endpoint. Also state plainly what an MT researcher gets: nobody removes this weight in deployment, and the compression link is dead in-repo (rtn+SW ≈ rtn). The MT-relevant payoff is B3's "is the decoder's constant the target-language selector," not B2.

**Hygiene.** "Generation collapses to repeated function words" rests on 2 models, 1 prompt, greedy (rule 3). Owen's "~7/16 unaffected" is cited as if about weights (rule 1). "Catastrophic" is a made-up cutoff the notes themselves disown. Cite Yu as ICLR-2025-rejected preprint consistently (bibliography says 2024; Subramanian cites 2025).

**Scoop risk.** Highest: mechanism-of-collapse in Llama — Cancedda (Meta), Sun/Kolter/Liu (CMU), Barbero/Yona/Gandelsman (DeepMind), Kempner (Fesser/Fel) all have the tooling and half the result. Medium: unit/calibrated null — Subramanian's group and Li 2026 (ICML) are adjacent. Safest: NLLB per-stack detection and the language-tag question — but Ferrando/Costa-jussà (UPC; ALTI+ and Queipo-de-Llano are theirs) are the one group positioned to do it, so move fast.

## (c) Cut and sharpen

Cut: question (2) as a headline; the drop-BOS arm; B2 at the collapsed endpoint; the "first table" framing (Owen already has a 16-model table of the activation intervention).

Sharpen: split the lesion into its two effects — the *constant* at position 0 versus the weight's *input-dependent contribution at every other position*. Yu Table 1 already says restoring the constant recovers only 42%, so the other 58% is the per-token pathway, and that is where H-prior lives. Position-restricted ablation via hook (zero W[j,k]·x_k at pos 0 only; at pos>0 only) is one afternoon and turns the mechanism question from "which of three stories" into a measured decomposition. Then carry that decomposition into NLLB's decoder.

## (d) Most valuable surviving question

Is a super weight's criticality carried by the constant it writes at the sink position or by its input-dependent contribution at every other position — and in NLLB's decoder, where position 0 is the target-language tag by construction, does that constant implement target-language selection?
